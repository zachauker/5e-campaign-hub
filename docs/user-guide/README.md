# User guide

How to use Campaign Hub, one guide per section. Written from the DM's seat: what
each screen is for, how to get it doing what you want, and what it does when
things go sideways.

You don't need to read these in order. If you're setting the app up for the first
time, start with [Getting started](getting-started.md); if a session starts in ten
minutes, read [Run mode](run-mode.md) and nothing else.

## Start here

- **[Getting started](getting-started.md)** — campaigns, the top bar, the command
  palette, and the handful of one-time setup steps worth doing before your first
  session.

## Prep

- **[Characters, locations, items & factions](entities.md)** — the campaign
  glossary. Filtering, the three view modes, quick-view popovers, and what lives on
  a detail page.
- **[Sessions](sessions.md)** — session notes read from your vault, linked to the
  places they happened in and pinned to the maps they happened on.
- **[Chronicle](chronicle.md)** — the in-world calendar, open threads, progress
  clocks, the timeline, and the session review that keeps them all current.
- **[Maps](maps.md)** — uploading your own maps, placing and styling pins, layers,
  sub-maps, and pinning session notes to the world.
- **[World map](world-map.md)** — the built-in vector map of Exandria.

## At the table

- **[Encounters](encounters.md)** — the initiative tracker. HP, conditions, stat
  blocks, monsters, and live PC sheets from D&D Beyond.
- **[Run mode](run-mode.md)** — keyboard-driven lookup, briefing cards, and
  one-key moment flagging while six people wait.

## Assistance

- **[Campaign assistant](assistant.md)** — asking questions about your own
  campaign, and the proposal system that keeps it from writing anything you didn't
  approve.
- **[Reference library](reference-library.md)** — the SRD and your own PDFs,
  searchable and citable, all of it staying on your machine.

## Connections & configuration

- **[Vault sync](vault-sync.md)** — pointing the hub at your Obsidian vault and
  keeping the two in step.
- **[Settings](settings.md)** — a tour of every switch on the settings page and
  what turning it on gets you.

---

## Conventions used in these guides

Keys are written as `⌘K` on Mac; use `Ctrl` on Windows and Linux. **Bold** marks
something you click or a label you'll see on screen.

Anything that spends money is called out where it appears — in practice that means
the campaign assistant, briefing builds, and session review, each of which uses
your own Anthropic API key. Nothing else in the app makes a paid call.
